Privacy Policy

Peach - Sum 10 Puzzle · Effective October 31, 2026 · Updated November 8, 2026

Cozyfield Works ("we") respects the privacy of everyone who uses Peach - Sum 10 Puzzle ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app handles. The app has no accounts or sign-up, and we never ask for your name, phone number, or email address.

1. Information we handle and why

InformationPurposeRetention
A random ID created by the app (identifies the device; not linked to your name or phone number)Finding your records and rankUntil you ask us to delete it

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use advertising or track you across other apps or websites.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the random ID above, your nickname, best score, recently loaded rankings, pre-loaded game boards, and any scores not yet sent while offline on your device. Unsent scores are sent to the server automatically once you are back online. Deleting the app removes this information from your device.

3. Sharing and international transfer

We do not share personal information with third parties or outsource its processing. The leaderboard server is operated by us in the Republic of Korea. If you use the app outside Korea, the information above is sent to and stored on our server in Korea.

4. Deletion

When the retention period ends or you ask for deletion, we promptly and permanently delete the related records from the server.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see or delete your information at any time. You can delete it right away with "Delete my nickname & records" on the ranking screen, or email us.

6. Children

The app does not collect information that could identify a child, such as a name or contact details. We ask players not to use real names or contact details as nicknames.

7. Security

Communication with the server is encrypted (HTTPS), and only we can access the server. We may keep server access logs for maintenance (time, requested address, and device type such as iPhone or Android), but we do not store IP addresses.
