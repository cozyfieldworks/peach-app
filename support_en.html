FAQ

My name doesn't show up in the ranking.
Set a nickname after finishing a game, and your score will be posted. An internet connection is required.

I want to delete my nickname or records.
Use "Delete my nickname & records" on the ranking screen, or email us.

I saw an offensive nickname.
Long-press the nickname in the ranking to report it, or email us. We will review and remove it.

Will my records move to a new phone?
If you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone/iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), your nickname and records carry over to your new phone. Otherwise, they stay on your current phone only.

When does "Today" reset?
Daily and weekly rankings reset at midnight Korea time (UTC+9).
